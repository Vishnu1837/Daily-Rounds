
MBBS 19-SUBJECT ROADMAP
Developer Flow-Chart Edition • NMC CBME 2024-aligned undergraduate scope
PURPOSE: A hierarchical content tree for an MBBS student app. Each arrow represents a child node that can be opened/expanded in the UI.
NODE MODEL: Phase → Subject → Main Section → Topic → Detailed Topic Nodes. The final nodes are syllabus/navigation labels, not explanatory textbook content.
REGULATORY BASIS: Revised NMC Competency Based Medical Education (CBME) Curriculum dated 12 September 2024. The four supplied roadmap drafts were used as the starting taxonomy; this edition adds one deeper undergraduate-level node layer.

NMC Phase-I alignment explicitly includes General Anatomy, upper/lower limb, abdomen, pelvis, thorax, head & neck, neuroanatomy, related embryology/histology/genetics; the NMC competency compendium reports 82 Anatomy topics and 413 Anatomy competencies, illustrating why a deeper expandable hierarchy is appropriate.
